## How delivery is approached

The [published process](https://ozairwebs.com/company/process/) describes collaboration from discovery through implementation and support. A practical scope identifies users, difficult workflows, existing systems, the desired outcome and who operates the result. Reviewable milestones should connect design decisions to working behaviour rather than a list of framework names.

Before launch, agree on testing, account ownership, backup and restore procedures, dependency licences and handover. After launch, assign responsibility for updates, operational alerts and content corrections. See [Why OzairWebs](https://ozairwebs.com/company/why-ozairwebs/) and the [company introduction](https://ozairwebs.com/company/about/) for the company's own published background. These statements are company information, not independent certifications.
